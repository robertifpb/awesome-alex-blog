Why I started this blog as a student developer

I wanted a place to document what I am learning, share small wins, and build the habit of finishing things in public instead of keeping every experiment private.

Learning web development can feel overwhelming because there is always another concept, another tool, and another project idea waiting. Starting this blog was my way to slow that down and give each step more visibility.

I do not want to wait until I feel like an expert before sharing what I build. Writing about the process helps me notice progress that would otherwise pass too quickly. It also turns small experiments into something more concrete and memorable.

What I hope this blog helps me practice

  • Writing clearly about technical topics in simple language.
  • Reflecting on what I learned instead of only moving to the next task.
  • Building consistency by publishing even small updates.

More than a portfolio

I see this blog as both a personal archive and a public learning space. Some posts will be technical notes. Others will be reflections about studying, building habits, or improving as a developer. Both are valuable because they show the process behind the final result.

If I keep publishing honestly, I think this space will become a useful record of how my skills and perspective changed over time. That feels like a good reason to keep going.
